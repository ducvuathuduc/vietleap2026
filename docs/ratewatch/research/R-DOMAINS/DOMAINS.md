## Part 1 — Domain sweep (26 domains)

Format per domain: **Insight hypothesis** (testable, decision-changing) · **Decision-maker** · **Data** (named source + URL) · **Hard join** (why hard to copy) · **Urgency** (dated event + URL).

**1. Capital markets / FTSE Russell upgrade.** Insight: the 21/9/2026 upgrade to secondary emerging market was "sell-the-news": on the first session after it, VN-Index fell 15.9 pts to 1,799.76 and foreigners net-sold VND664bn ($25.5m) on HoSE, led by VIC -2.57% / VHM -4.08% [verified, https://theinvestor.vn/foreign-investors-remain-net-sellers-after-vietnam-market-status-upgrade-d20064.html]; foreign selling continued (~$1bn net sold in Oct 2026 [verified, repo DATA_SOURCES.md citing VnEconomy]) while margin debt hit a record 457.5tn VND in Q3/2026 [verified, repo DATA_SOURCES.md citing VnEconomy] — i.e., foreign exit is being funded by leveraged retail. Decision-maker: domestic retail investors, asset managers, SSC. Data: FiinQuant foreign-flow series 2021+ and margin/valuation sets (already in repo, 53,049 foreign-flow rows), plus the article above. Hard join: foreign-flow × margin-debt × VN30-membership panel around two event dates (announcement Oct 2025, effective 21/9/2026). Urgency: upgrade effective 21/9/2026 (URL above); Feb 2026 foreign-access reform allowing purchases via international brokerages [verified, AFP wire 04/02/2026 "Vietnam gives foreign investors easier access to stock market", read in wire snippet].

**2. Deposits and real interest rates.** Insight: 2026 is a deposit-rate war in reverse — average new-deposit rates rose >1pp in 2026 (SBV briefing 7/10/2026: 6.38%, +1.15pp YTD [inferred, https://ginity.vn/en/blog/vietnam-rates-fx-late-2026]), credit outran deposits and NIM compressed [verified, same URL meta], so SBV ordered ≥6-month deposit rates cut ≥0.5pp (Document 9608/NHNN-CSTT, 18/10/2026 [inferred, https://www.realtique.net/en/news/market-update/45115/investing-in-vietnam-2026-savings-gold-stocks-rental-property/]) while Oct CPI ran 5.09% — real deposit rates near zero exactly when households hold a record 10.8 quadrillion VND in banks (end-May 2026 [inferred, https://en.vietnamplus.vn/household-savings-hit-record-as-high-interest-rates-attract-idle-money-post349264.vnp]). Decision-maker: households choosing deposits vs gold vs margin; SBV. Data: SBV rate documents, NSO CPI (in repo), FiinQuant bank ratios (614 rows in repo). Hard join: deposit-rate policy events × CPI item × bank-level NIM. Urgency: Document 9608/NHNN-CSTT 18/10/2026 (URL above).

**3. Gold and FX.** Insight: the domestic gold premium (~14% over world price; SJC bar 141.7m VND/lượng ≈ $5,484 on 24/10/2026 vs world ~$4,008/oz on 27/10 [inferred, https://www.riotimesonline.com/vietnam-gold-price-sjc-premium-2026/ and https://www.vietnam.vn/en/cap-nhat-gia-vang-sang-27-10-2026-sjc-nhan-tron-9999-doji-btmc-phu-quy]) is a shadow price of financial repression: it should widen when deposit rates are forced down and Fed hikes pull USD rates up (Fed hiked Sept 2026, first since 2023 [verified, AFP wire 16/09/2026]; gold fell under $4,000 in June 2026 on rate expectations [verified, AFP wire 24/06/2026]). Vietnam still has no national gold exchange; bars trade at a premium [verified, AFP wire 30/01/2026 "Heavy metal: soaring gold price a crushing weight in Vietnam"]. Decision-maker: households (buy gold now?), SBV (liberalisation pace). Data: daily SJC/ring quotes on vietnam.vn price pages (robots-allowed), world XAU, SBV interbank rate (25,964 VND/USD end-Sept 2026, -1.17% YTD [inferred, https://www.vietnam.vn/en/diem-sang-ty-gia-noi-dai-chuoi-ha-nhiet-huong-toi-thang-thu-sau-giam-gia-lien-tiep]). Urgency: premium at ~14% while SBV cuts deposit rates (Oct-Nov 2026).

Decision-maker: MOF/CAA, province planners, contractors. Data: MOF Report 908/BC-BTC Appendix Ia (34 provinces + 33 central agencies, already in repo, 133 rows), CAA portal (in repo), HDX COD-AB 34-province boundaries (in repo). ## Part 3 — Red-team of top 4 (World Bank economist + FiinGroup analyst)

**A. FTSE sell-the-news (rank 1).** Strongest objection (FiinGroup): day-1 flows prove nothing — foreign selling predates the upgrade, the event was announced in Oct 2025 and is fully priced; attributing 21/9 moves to the upgrade is event-study contamination, and FiinQuant foreign flows exclude some trade types. Second objection (WB): passive-inflow estimates were never a promise; Fed hikes (Sept 2026) confound. Fatal?

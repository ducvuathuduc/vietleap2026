## Market observations

The 12-month query uses 20 November 2025–19 November 2026 inclusive in Vietnam time. The latest close is the unadjusted close in VND per share. P/B is a multiple and carries its own observation date. Daily volatility uses the sample standard deviation of the last 60 observed adjusted-close log returns multiplied by √252 and 100. That requires 61 positive closing observations. Drawdown is the percentage change from the running peak of the adjusted close within the requested window. Maximum drawdown is the most negative observation, with its trough date. A later-starting bank therefore has a shorter drawdown history. No forward fill is applied.

Foreign net flow sums the vendor's daily net buy value in VND over the last 20 observed close sessions for each bank. The sum requires all 20 values. A positive value means net purchases. Foreign room is the remaining number of shares that foreign investors may buy under the reported foreign ownership limit. The per-bank summary includes observation dates and non-null counts. Daily peer-level medians and net-flow totals include source coverage by date. [FiinQuant historical bars](https://docs.fiinquant.vn/fiinquant-en/function-and-formula/historical-data-function) · [Trading and foreign endpoint](https://apigw.fiingroup.vn/FXMA/TradingData/GetTradingDataByMultiEntities) · [Valuation endpoint](https://apigw.fiingroup.vn/FXMA/MarketInDepth/valuation/GetValuation) · [Foreign field definitions](https://docs.fiinquant.vn/ham-va-cong-thuc/1.-danh-muc-and-thong-tin-co-ban/1.5.-lay-du-lieu-room-ndtnn) · [Market summary](aggregates/bank_market_daily_summary.csv) · [Coverage](aggregates/bank_market_coverage.csv) · [Daily peer aggregates](aggregates/bank_market_daily_peer_aggregates.csv)

## Reproduction

Run the scripts with the repository's Python venv from the repository root, with bytecode disabled. Run `collect.py` for the ratio and market pulls, `probe_fundamentals.py screen_all` for ROA, and `probe_fundamentals.py capital` for the book equity proxy. Then run `join_rates.py` and `verify.py`. The statement and asset-screener controls are `probe_fundamentals.py old` and `probe_fundamentals.py assets`. The public source audit is `check_sources.py`.

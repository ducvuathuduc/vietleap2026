# R-DEMAND — What decision to serve, and where the white space is

Access date for every source below: **20 November 2026**.  
Labels: **[verified]** = this worker read the page or the search extract of that URL on that date. **[inferred]** = a conclusion, not a printed number. No figure below was estimated to fill a gap. Conflicting official counts are left side by side.

**Recommendation.** Build for the household-business owner standing on the **1 billion VND/year cliff** (tax + e-invoice + compulsory social insurance together). That is the decision ordinary people are making in Q4 2026, it is a tax-compliance question for the World Bank judge, and it is not the FiinQuant stock dashboard other teams will ship. A plain tax calculator already exists (MISA); the white space is the joint obligation and the national picture of who sits under the line.

---

## 1–2. Long list and what already exists

Sixteen decisions. “How many” is only a figure this worker actually read. Where the count was not found, the cell says so.

### A. Taxes — household business (the live 2026 reform)

**A1. Stay under 1 tỷ or cross it and start paying VAT and PIT.**  
- Who: household and individual businesses. Tax authority count via the Government portal: about **2,556,042** households/individuals with annual revenue under 1 billion VND. [verified] [Chinhphu, 29/04/2026](https://baochinhphu.vn/chinh-thuc-nang-nguong-chiu-thue-voi-ho-kinh-doanh-len-01-ty-dong-nam-ap-dung-tu-1-1-2026-102260429185517215.htm).  
- Wider population, not the same statistic: Politburo Resolution 68-NQ/TW (04/05/2025), as quoted by a legal site, says the private sector had **more than 5 million** active household businesses. [verified as a quotation, resolution PDF not opened] [thuviennhadat](https://thuviennhadat.vn/phap-ly-nha-dat/so-luong-doanh-nghiep-va-ho-kinh-doanh-dang-hoat-dong-trong-khu-vuc-kinh-te-tu-nhan-hien-nay-677371.html). Preliminary 2026 economic census briefing: **nearly 5.3 million** non-farm individual establishments and **859,048** active enterprises at 31/12/2025. [verified as press citing the 13/07/2026 briefing] [doanhnghiephoinhap](https://doanhnghiephoinhap.vn/tong-dieu-tra-kinh-te-2026-doanh-nghiep-dan-dat-tang-truong-kinh-te-ca-the-lan-dau-giam-lao-dong-143102.html). A VCCI/PCI write-up says about **6.1 million** household businesses at end-2025. [verified as press] [VOV](https://vov.vn/kinh-te/755-doanh-nghiep-ket-von-26-van-phai-chiu-chi-phi-khong-chinh-thuc-post1292848.vov). These three counts conflict; do not average them.  
- Decision: declare revenue, and whether this year’s sales cross the line that turns tax on.  
- Why now: presumptive tax ends 01/01/2026 (Resolution 198/2025/QH15, as cited in the tax press). Decree 68/2026/NĐ-CP (05/03/2026) set a 500 million line; Decree **141/2026/NĐ-CP (29/04/2026, in force 01/01/2026)** replaced “500 million” with **1 billion** for VAT, PIT and the e-invoice rule. Households at or under 1 tỷ who already paid under Decree 68 get the excess handled under Article 12. Budget effect stated by the Ministry of Finance on that page: about **16,650 billion VND** less revenue than 2025 (old 100 million line and presumptive tax) and about **4,850 billion** less than the 500 million policy. [verified] same Chinhphu URL.  
- Band shape, later and coarser: at a VCCI workshop on 21/08/2026, a finance-ministry official said 2025 tax data showed **2.7 million** households under 1 tỷ (**more than 96%** of tax-paying households), **2.84%** in the 1–3 tỷ band, **0.73%** in 3–10 tỷ, **0.14%** above 10 tỷ. [verified as the article extract] [Tuổi Trẻ](https://tuoitre.vn/khi-nang-nguong-len-10-ti-dong-hon-99-ho-kinh-doanh-tinh-thue-theo-doanh-thu-100260821142425173.htm). An earlier ministry dossier, quoted by VnExpress, said about **3.6 million** households under tax management, **1.8 million** under 500 million, **about 37,000** over 1 billion and **over 4,000** over 10 billion. [verified as the article extract; the dossier’s own date was not on the extract] [VnExpress](https://vnexpress.net/thue-nguong-nao-moi-la-nguong-cuoi-5066234.html). Do not treat 37,000 and the August 2026 percentages as one time series.  
- Policy question: does the 1 tỷ cliff bunch declared revenue just underneath it, and is the revenue loss (4,850 / 16,650 billion) buying compliance or just a higher exemption?  
- Data that would answer it: quarterly declared-revenue histogram from the tax authority, before vs after 01/01/2026. Not public at invoice level. Published bands above are the first-pass substitute.  
- Tools: [MISA eShop household-tax calculator](https://www.misaeshop.vn/cong-cu-tinh-thue-hkd/) and [MISA’s 2026 explainer](https://helpeshop.misa.vn/kb/cach-tinh-thue-ho-kinh-doanh-2026-moi-nhat-theo-luat-thue-tncn-thue-gtgt-sua-doi) already do the arithmetic (revenue-ratio vs 15% on profit for the 1–3 tỷ band; rates by industry in the explainer). [VNPT HKD](https://hkd.vnpt.vn/Account/LandingPage) sells e-invoice + filing. eTax Mobile is reported in wide use (below). **Gap:** none of these put tax, the 30-day invoice clock and social insurance on one screen, and none show the national band chart a judge can argue from. The MISA page extract also shows a sample that labels **800,000,000 VND** as “group 2 (1–3 billion)”, which is internally inconsistent. [verified as search extract, not a full product test] — treat vendor calculators as unverified until clicked through.  
- Served? The arithmetic is **partly served**. The decision product is not. Strong candidate.

**A2. Register a coded e-invoice within 30 days of crossing 1 tỷ.**  
- Who: same households, plus anyone selling an asset that must be registered. Above 1 tỷ, coded e-invoice or a cash-register e-invoice connected to the tax authority is mandatory; at or under 1 tỷ it is optional. New businesses, and businesses whose prior year was not above 1 tỷ, must register **within 30 days of the end of the tax period in which cumulative revenue exceeds 1 tỷ**. [verified] Decree 141 text on the Chinhphu page above; also [Chinhphu guidance for under-1-tỷ households, 23/07/2026 tax letter](https://xaydungchinhsach.chinhphu.vn/huong-dan-su-dung-hoa-don-dien-tu-voi-ho-kinh-doanh-co-doanh-thu-duoi-1-ty-dong-nam-119260727150121544.htm) and Decree 254/2026/NĐ-CP (30/06/2026) as summarized there.  
- How many have already registered a cash-register e-invoice: **110,295** by 30/06/2025 (more than 20,000 of them presumptive-tax households under 1 tỷ, voluntary) and **315,260** by 15/06/2026 (+56% vs 31/12/2025). On-time filing **98.1%** by 23/06/2026; **2.72 million** households (stated as 86%) on eTax Mobile. [verified as printed in Tạp chí Tài chính Doanh nghiệp, 24/07/2026; the underlying tax-authority release was not opened] [TCDN](https://taichinhdoanhnghiep.net.vn/quan-ly-thue-doi-voi-ho-kinh-doanh-o-viet-nam-thuc-tien-chuyen-doi-tu-thue-khoan-sang-ke-khai-d68488.html).  
- Decision: register now, or wait until the 30-day clock starts. Shops that sell direct to consumers (retail except cars/motorbikes, food, lodging, passenger transport, entertainment) are pushed toward cash-register invoices. [verified as a legal-site summary of Decree 254] [thuvienphapluat](https://thuvienphapluat.vn/phap-luat-doanh-nghiep/cong-viec-phap-ly/dang-ky-su-dung-hoa-don-dien-tu-ho-kinh-doanh-ca-nhan-kinh-doanh-tu-1-7-2026-382.html).  
- Policy question: is invoice coverage stuck on the minority above 1 tỷ (315 thousand registrations against ~2.56 million under the line), and should the voluntary under-1-tỷ path be free everywhere, not only in poor areas?  
- Tools: VNPT Invoice-POS, MISA, Viettel and other providers sell the invoice. Tax portal accepts registration. **Gap:** no tool tells a shop “you are N đồng and M days from the legal trigger.” Strong add-on to A1, not a separate product.

**A3. Stay a household or become a company, now that companies at or under 1 tỷ are also CIT-exempt.**  
- Who: the same owners, plus about **235,800** companies the Ministry of Finance says fall under the new exemption, worth about **2,164 billion VND** of CIT. [verified] same Chinhphu Decree 141 article. Exemption does not apply to subsidiaries or related parties that fail the test.  
- Decision: convert, because Resolution 68 wants household businesses to become companies and the tax penalty for doing so was just removed at this revenue level.  
- Policy question: does equalising the 1 tỷ exemption actually raise the company count toward the Resolution 68 target of 2 million firms by 2030, or do owners stay households to avoid social insurance, invoicing and accounting?  
- Tools: incorporation services are everywhere. **Gap:** nobody shows the owner the three-way cost (household tax vs CIT-exempt company vs the social-insurance bill in B1). Strong analytical add-on, easy to fake if the cost model is invented — only use published rates.

**A4. Salaried worker: register dependents and recompute 2026 PIT before year-end.**  
- Who: resident individuals with wages. A headcount of PIT filers was **not found** this pass.  
- Decision: the personal deduction is **15.5 million VND/month (186 million/year)** and **6.2 million per dependent**. Resolution **110/2025/UBTVQH15** (passed 17/10/2025, in force 01/01/2026, applies from tax year 2026) [verified] [Chinhphu](https://xaydungchinhsach.chinhphu.vn/nghi-quyet-110-2025-ubtvqh15-dieu-chinh-muc-giam-tru-gia-canh-cua-thue-thu-nhap-ca-nhan-119251110101313787.htm). Law **109/2025/QH15** (10/12/2025, in force 01/07/2026; wage rules apply from tax year 2026) writes the same deductions into statute and lets the Government set a deduction for the taxpayer’s own medical and education spending. [verified] [Chinhphu law record](https://vanban.chinhphu.vn/?docid=216495&pageid=27160) and [Chinhphu, 31/03/2026](https://xaydungchinhsach.chinhphu.vn/quy-dinh-giam-tru-gia-canh-trong-thue-thu-nhap-ca-nhan-119260327071750084.htm). The tax department’s 10/12/2025 note says the schedule cuts the second bracket from 15% to **10%** and the third from 25% to **20%**, and the law passed 438/443 deputies (92.6%). [verified] [GDT news](https://www.gdt.gov.vn/wps/portal/home/news/detail?1dmy=&current=true&urile=wcm%3Apath%3A%2Fgdt+content%2Fsa_gdt%2Fsa_news%2Fsa_news_economy%2F2025%2Fthang+12%2Fb2b6d1c0-dede-4cc1-b953-bb3b8d53d229). The **income cut-points** of each bracket were not in that note. Do not invent them.  
- Why now: November 2026 is inside the first tax year of the new deduction. Medical/education relief cannot be promised until the Government’s cap is read; this pass did not find that cap.  
- Policy question: how many wage earners are pulled out of PIT by 15.5 / 6.2, and is the new medical/education deduction usable or still an empty article?  
- Tools: explanations are abundant (the two official pages above). A plain calculator is a **weak, well-served** idea. Building one without the full bracket table from the law PDF would be wrong.

**A5. VAT on a small online import, and the 2-point VAT cut through end-2026.**  
- Who: people and small traders buying low-value goods by express delivery. No buyer count found.  
- Decision: the landed price. From **18/02/2025**, express consignments are no longer VAT-exempt (Decision 01/2025/QĐ-TTg). Customs told the Government portal that by **15/09/2025** this had raised **1,082 billion VND**. [verified] [Chinhphu, 04/12/2025](https://baochinhphu.vn/nhung-diem-moi-ve-thue-gia-tri-gia-tang-thue-tieu-thu-dac-biet-va-tac-dong-toi-doanh-nghiep-102251204132038935.htm). Separately, a 2-point VAT cut on many 10% goods runs **01/07/2025 through 31/12/2026** (Resolution 204/2025/QH15, Decree 174/2025/NĐ-CP), with finance, real estate, metals and excisable goods excluded. [verified as a legal-site summary] [thuvienphapluat](https://thuvienphapluat.vn/chinh-sach-phap-luat-moi/vn/ho-tro-phap-luat/chinh-sach-moi/99961/tong-hop-nhung-diem-moi-ve-thue-gtgt-thue-tncn-tu-2026).  
- Policy question: did ending the small-parcel exemption shift spending back to domestic sellers, or only raise the tax take by 1,082 billion in seven months?  
- Urgency is real but **older** (February 2025) than the October–November 2026 shocks. Tools: Shopee/platform checkout shows a fee; no independent landed-cost tool was opened. Medium candidate, easy to look like a shopping calculator.

### B. Social insurance, health insurance, pension

**B1. Household owner: compulsory social insurance or not, and at what wage base.**  
- Who: registered household owners. From 01/07/2025, owners who paid tax by declaration had to join (Decree 158/2025/NĐ-CP, 25/06/2025). From **01/01/2026** the gate changed: compulsory only if registered, declared revenue **over 1 tỷ**, and actually liable for VAT and PIT. Owners at or under 1 tỷ may join **voluntary** insurance. Everyone else who is registered waits until **01/07/2029**. [verified] [Chinhphu, 13/09/2026](https://xaydungchinhsach.chinhphu.vn/quy-dinh-ve-tham-gia-bhxh-bat-buoc-doi-voi-chu-ho-kinh-doanh-11926091307434634.htm) and [Ministry of Finance Q&A](https://isa.mof.gov.vn/hoidapcstc/home/cthoidap/163501).  
- Contribution: the owner chooses the wage base, floor = reference wage = base salary, cap = 20 times that, rate **29.5%** (22% retirement/death, 3% sickness/maternity, 4.5% health). [verified] [BHXH Việt Nam, article dated 17/11/2025](https://baohiemxahoi.gov.vn/tintuc/Pages/linh-vuc-bao-hiem-xa-hoi.aspx?CateID=168&ItemID=25698). That same article, dated **17/11/2025** (twelve months before this note), says **more than 12,300** such owners were enrolled and the average base was about **3.5 million VND/month**, with the then floor 2,340,000 producing a minimum of **690,300 VND/month**. Do **not** quote 12,300 as the November 2026 coverage. No later enrollment count was found.  
- Base salary from **01/07/2026** is **2,530,000 VND/month** (Decree 161/2026/NĐ-CP, 15/05/2026). [verified via Nhân Dân citing BHXH letter 1819 and the decree, and via a legal-site restatement; the Công báo page confirmed the decree’s existence and date but the amount was not on the extract] [Nhân Dân](https://nhandan.vn/ba-nhom-doi-tuong-dong-bao-hiem-xa-hoi-bao-hiem-y-te-bao-hiem-that-nghiep-theo-luong-co-so-moi-post967687.html), [Công báo](https://congbao.chinhphu.vn/van-ban/nghi-dinh-so-161-2026-nd-cp-469550.htm).  
- National system, end-September 2026: **22.64 million** social-insurance participants (20.05 million compulsory, 2.59 million voluntary), **18.06 million** unemployment insurance, **98.64 million** health insurance. [verified] [BHXH open statistics](https://baohiemxahoi.gov.vn/congkhai/pages/so-lieu-thong-ke-nganh.aspx?CateID=130&ItemID=27254).  
- Decision: if the shop is over 1 tỷ, pick a base between the floor and 20× the floor. If under, voluntary or nothing until 2029.  
- Policy question: the 2026 tax change **narrowed** the compulsory gate to the over-1-tỷ minority. Is that leaving most owners uncovered until 2029, and are those who do join anchoring on a base near the old 3.5 million (a pension that stays small)?  
- Tools: the BHXH site explains the rule. VssID pays benefits. **No calculator was found that takes the shop’s revenue and returns “you are / are not compulsory, and here is the floor in đồng after 01/07/2026.”** This is the unserved half of A1. Strong candidate, but only with a fresh enrollment number or with the 12,300 figure clearly dated November 2025.

**B2. Family health-insurance renewal, and a student’s tuition plus student health insurance for 2026–27.**  
- Who: household health-insurance buyers and university students. Participant stock is the 98.64 million above. Student headcount not fetched.  
- Decision: from 01/07/2026 the household premium is 4.5% of the new base salary for the first person, then 70/60/50/40% for the next members. [verified as the legal-site restatement of the rule] [thuvienphapluat](https://thuvienphapluat.vn/chinh-sach-phap-luat-moi/vn/ho-tro-phap-luat/chinh-sach-moi/112515/2-muc-luong-co-so-ap-dung-nam-2026). Students: the state pays at least half; the student’s half is **56,925 VND/month, 683,100 VND/year**. [verified] [Chinhphu](https://xaydungchinhsach.chinhphu.vn/muc-dong-bao-hiem-y-te-hoc-sinh-sinh-vien-nam-hoc-2026-2027-119260822112008148.htm). Co-pay stops, after 5 continuous years, once the patient’s co-pay in the year exceeds 6× the reference wage. [verified as statute text] [consolidated Health Insurance Law](https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/Bao-hiem/Van-ban-hop-nhat-40-VBHN-VPQH-2026-Luat-Bao-hiem-y-te-699508.aspx).  
- Tuition ceilings for public universities that do not cover their own recurrent costs, school year 2026–27, Decree 238/2025/NĐ-CP: from **1,710** (arts) to **3,500** thousand VND per student per month (medicine/pharmacy); autonomous universities up to 2× or 2.5×. [verified as the legal-site table of the decree] [thuvienphapluat](https://thuvienphapluat.vn/chinh-sach-phap-luat-moi/vn/ho-tro-phap-luat/tu-van-phap-luat/119015/quy-dinh-ve-hoc-phi-doi-voi-giao-duc-dai-hoc-nam-hoc-2026-2027-nhu-the-nao). MOET letter 7526/BGDĐT-KHTC (2026) tells universities not to jump fees. [verified as the letter’s existence on that site].  
- Policy question: after the base-salary rise, who drops household health insurance, and do the Decree 238 ceilings still get breached by autonomous universities?  
- Tools: BHXH’s own payment channels. University sites list their own fees. **A national “what will my family pay” page was not opened.** Usable but dull, and the fee tables are already published. Weak wow.

### C. Savings, gold, securities, consumer credit

**C1. Buy gold after the 5-million-a-day non-cash rule.**  
- Who: any household buying gold. A count of buyers was **not found**. Do not invent one.  
- Decision: from **16/10/2026**, if one customer’s gold purchases and sales in a day total **5 million VND or more**, payment must be non-cash. The threshold is the **day’s total**, not each ticket, and splitting to dodge it is a violation. It was **20 million** under Decree 232. [verified] Decree **390/2026/NĐ-CP** (16/10/2026) as explained by the State Bank on [Chinhphu](https://baochinhphu.vn/lam-ro-nguong-thanh-toan-khong-dung-tien-mat-khi-mua-ban-vang-102261027160416521.htm) and the decree notice [Chinhphu, 17/10/2026](https://baochinhphu.vn/tu-16-10-2026-mua-ban-vang-trong-ngay-tu-5-trieu-dong-phai-thanh-toan-khong-dung-tien-mat-102261017172716946.htm). The 5 million line matches the non-cash line already used for VAT (Decree 181/2025) and CIT (Decree 320/2025), per the State Bank’s reply.  
- Also still in force: Decree **232/2025/NĐ-CP** (26/08/2025, in force 10/10/2025) ended the state’s monopoly on gold-bar minting. Enterprises need 1,000 billion VND of charter capital and a bar-trading licence; commercial banks need 50,000 billion. [verified] [SBV summary](https://sbv.gov.vn/vi/w/n%E1%BB%99i-dung-c%C6%A1-b%E1%BA%A3n-c%E1%BB%A7a-ngh%E1%BB%8B-%C4%91%E1%BB%8Bnh-s%E1%BB%91-232/2025/n%C4%91-cp-v%E1%BB%81-qu%E1%BA%A3n-l%C3%BD-ho%E1%BA%A1t-%C4%91%E1%BB%99ng-kinh-doanh-v%C3%A0ng), [Chinhphu announcement](https://baochinhphu.vn/chinh-thuc-xoa-bo-co-che-nha-nuoc-doc-quyen-san-xuat-vang-mieng-102250826145041005.htm).  
- Why now: five weeks old. A small jewelry purchase that used to be cash is now a bank transfer plus an invoice.  
- Policy question: does cutting the cash threshold from 20 million to 5 million pull gold into the formal payment system, or push small buyers to unlicensed shops?  
- Tools: price boards are widespread; this pass did not audit them. **Gap:** a page that says “this purchase crosses 5 million, so it must be a transfer, and here is the deposit alternative.” The price itself is well served. The rule is not. Good consumer demo, thinner as “data analysis” unless SBV’s reported gold prices are actually in the pipeline.

**C2. Leave money in the bank, buy gold, or open a securities account.**  
- Who: savers. Securities accounts at **31/10/2026**, VSDC via the finance ministry paper: **14,384,902** total, of which **14,311,172** domestic individuals. [verified as the newspaper citing VSDC; the VSDC index page lists an October 2026 release updated 05/11/2026 but the PDF was not opened] [Thời báo Tài chính](https://thoibaotaichinhvietnam.vn/so-luong-tai-khoan-nha-dau-tu-dat-14-4-trieu-tai-khoan-tinh-den-cuoi-thang-10-2026-206477.html), [VSDC index](https://vsdc.vn/vi/alc/84).  
- Decision: deposit interest at credit institutions (VND, gold or foreign currency; demand, term, savings, certificates) is **PIT-exempt with no cap**, Decree **253/2026/NĐ-CP** in force 01/07/2026. [verified as the decree text on a legal consolidator] [vanban.org Article 24](https://vanban.org/van-ban/nghi-dinh-253-2026-nd-cp-bien-phap-luat-thue-thu-nhap-ca-nhan/dieu-24). A posted deposit rate for November 2026 was **not fetched**. Do not invent one.  
- Policy question: with 14.3 million individual accounts and a still-exempt deposit rate, are households taking gold and equity risk they do not understand, and should the interest exemption stay?  
- Tools: every bank app, every broker, FiinQuant (launched as a stock-data library, 06/06/2025) [verified] [FiinPro](https://fiinpro.com/News/Detail/11463535?lang=en-US). **This is the most crowded cell in the contest.** Weak candidate. See section 3.

**C3. Take a consumer loan from a licensed app or not.**  
- Who: people without collateral. No borrower count fetched.  
- Decision: finance-company consumer loans are still capped at **100 million VND** total outstanding per customer under Circular 43/2016 (cars excluded). On **23/10/2025** the State Bank asked for comments (letter 9294/NHNN-CSTT) on raising that cap to **400 million**. [verified] [VNBA](https://vnba.org.vn/vi/vnba-lay-y-kien-hoi-vien-ve-du-thao-sua-doi--bo-sung-thong-tu-43-19453.htm), [Thời báo Tài chính](https://thoibaotaichinhvietnam.vn/ngan-hang-nha-nuoc-de-xuat-tang-han-muc-cho-vay-tieu-dung-gap-4-lan-len-400-trieu-dong-186202.html). This pass found **no enacted circular** that replaces the 100 million cap. Absence of a search hit is not proof none exists. E-KYC life-need loans are also described, on a commercial explainer, as capped at 100 million per bank under Circular 06/2023. [verified only as that site’s claim] [huongdanvay.com](https://huongdanvay.com/quy-trinh-vay/vay-tien-bang-cccd-gan-chip).  
- Why it is less urgent than A1 or C1: the painful change is still a **draft from October 2025**.  
- Policy question: does the 100 million cap push borrowers to unlicensed apps?  
- Tools: bank blogs list licensed apps [Techcombank, updated 19/11/2026 on the page](https://techcombank.com/thong-tin/blog/app-vay-tien-nhanh). Comparison sites exist. **Well served as marketing, poorly served as a total-cost comparison a regulator would trust.** Medium, and easy to become an ad page. Skip for a 26-hour build unless a licensed APR table is already in hand.

**C4. Hand biometrics to someone who calls about “updating the bank”.**  
- Who: anyone with a payment account. No victim count fetched.  
- Rule that created the pretext: Circular **17/2024/TT-NHNN** — from 01/07/2025 an organisation’s account cannot move money electronically until the legal representative’s biometrics match the chip ID or VNeID. [verified] [Chinhphu](https://xaydungchinhsach.chinhphu.vn/tu-1-7-tam-dung-giao-dich-tai-khoan-doanh-nghiep-neu-chua-cap-nhat-sinh-trac-hoc-119250524104455679.htm). Decision 2345 (transfers over 10 million from 01/07/2024) is the pretext named in scam warnings. [verified] [Ministry of Agriculture ATTT page](https://attt.mae.gov.vn/Pages/mao-danh-ngan-hang-huong-dan-nguoi-dung-xac-minh-sinh-trac-hoc.aspx). ## 5. Top 3 concepts

### 1. Ngưỡng 1 tỷ (build this)

- **User:** the owner of a registered shop, stall or small service business.  
- **Question:** “If my revenue this year is X, what must I pay, what must I register, and by when — and how many shops like mine are in this band?”  
- **Decision it supports:** stay under the line, cross it and register an e-invoice within 30 days, pick a social-insurance wage base, or convert to a company that is CIT-exempt at the same 1 tỷ line.  
- **Demo moment:** start at 980 million. The screen shows no VAT/PIT, invoice optional, social insurance voluntary. Change one field to 1.05 billion. VAT/PIT turn on, a 30-day invoice deadline appears, compulsory insurance appears at the legal floor. Beside it, the Ministry of Finance bands: more than 96% of tax-paying households are under 1 tỷ (August 2026 statement) and the exemption costs about 4,850 billion versus the 500 million policy (Government portal, 29/04/2026).  
- **Policy line the evidence can support, if the chart is real:** the 2026 reform stacked three duties on the same 1 tỷ notch (Decree 141, Decree 254, the social-insurance gate as restated on 13/09/2026). If declared revenue bunches just under that notch, the notch is a compliance cliff and should be smoothed. **Bunching is not yet proven.** The 2.84% / 0.73% / 0.14% bands are too wide to show it. The product’s job is to show only the published bands, and to say what microdata would be required.  
- **Kill risk:** MISA, VNPT and eTax already file the return, so a judge can call a pure calculator a form. The demo dies if the national band chart is dropped, or if the team presents the November 2025 figure of 12,300 insured owners as if it were today’s coverage. It also dies if two conflicting household counts are blended into one number.

### 2. Five-million-đồng gold (the consumer alternative)

- **User:** a household buying a small amount of gold, or choosing between gold and a bank deposit.  
- **Question:** “Does this purchase have to leave the cash economy, and is a deposit still tax-free?”  
- **Decision:** pay by transfer and keep the invoice once the day’s gold reaches 5 million (Decree 390, 16/10/2026), and compare with a deposit whose interest is PIT-exempt with no cap (Decree 253, from 01/07/2026).  
- **Demo moment:** a purchase of a few triệu flips from “cash allowed” to “non-cash, and splitting the tickets is itself a violation,” next to the old 20 million line it replaced on 16/10/2026.  
- **Policy line:** the State Bank set 5 million to match the VAT/CIT non-cash line and to see gold flows. The evidence can support a recommendation to publish, monthly, the share of licensed gold turnover that is non-cash before and after 16/10/2026. It cannot yet say the rule worked: that share was not found.  
- **Kill risk:** it looks like a legal FAQ plus a gold-price widget. Price data may be restricted. Without a cited deposit rate the comparison is an empty box. Judges who just left a dashboard workshop may score it as thin.

### 3. The 2% power cut, by tier (only if the annex is read first)

- **User:** a household or a small shop with a meter.  
- **Question:** “The average price fell 2% on 1 November. Did my tier fall 2%?”  
- **Decision:** whether to move use out of the peak hours in Decision 963, or to treat the bill as essentially unchanged.  
- **Demo moment:** two columns from Decision 2892 — a low-tier household and a peak-hour shop — so the audience sees the average is not the bill. **Do not build this until the PDF tiers are transcribed.** The legal-site extract was ambiguous.  
- **Policy line:** if the low tiers fell by less than the high tiers (or the reverse), a flat 2% announcement is the wrong way to describe a progressive tariff. The VPCP notice of 27/10/2026 framed it as support for households and firms; the test is whether the annex does that.  
- **Kill risk:** EVN and the local power companies can already answer “what is my price,” the change is a cut rather than a shock, and four energy mentors make this a likely neighbour-team project. Missing kWh-by-tier data leaves the incidence claim [inferred] forever.

**Do not lead with** a securities or macro dashboard (section 3), a PIT calculator (bracket cut-points not yet copied; explainers exist), CBAM (right for Dray and Đoàn Vũ Nguyên, wrong for “anyone”), or farm insurance (right subsidy, no public premium, so the number would have to be invented).

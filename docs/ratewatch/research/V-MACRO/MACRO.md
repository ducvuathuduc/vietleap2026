## 5. The rate the saver needs

[inferred] **Forecast method: none.** Posted retail deposit yields are bank funding offers. Regulation, funding mix, maturity preferences, liquidity, credit and offer eligibility affect the spread across maturities. Savers cannot lock or trade the second-leg quote today. The observed O/N-to-9M interbank curve is a separate wholesale market with sparse longer-turnover observations. These conditions support a break-even comparison and do not identify an expected retail rate.

For principal `P`, annualized decimal rates `r6` and `r12`, and year fractions `d1`, `d2`, `d12` using the bank's day-count rule, a bullet payout and reinvestment of all first-leg interest give `P(1+r6*d1)(1+r_next*d2)` versus `P(1+r12*d12)`. Solve `r_next* = ((1+r12*d12)/(1+r6*d1) − 1)/d2`. Above the threshold, two deposits earn more, assuming unchanged eligibility and same endpoint. Below it, the locked deposit earns more. For exact display use actual dates, payable cash flows, renewal product terms and rounding. At both maturities check eligible principal plus interest across the saver’s balances with the same legal institution, including other accounts. The current insurance cap is VND 350 million per person per participating institution from 13 July 2026, including insured principal and interest. Show dated NPL, coverage, capital or LDR as reported financial indicators. [DIV notice, verified in B-POLICY at 12:17:13](https://www.div.gov.vn/tu-ngay-13-7-2026-han-muc-chi-tra-tien-bao-hiem-cua-bao-hiem-tien-gui-viet-nam-la-350-trieu-dong)

[verified calculation] [VietinBank's public retail HTML schedule](https://www.vietinbank.vn/ca-nhan/cong-cu-tien-ich/lai-suat-khcn) (200 at 12:43:22) showed 3.00% for the 6-to-<7M bracket and 5.40% for 12M. With half-years and simple interest, the second six-month rate must be **7.685%/year** to tie. Renewal at the same 3.00% would earn **3.0225%** over the year versus **5.40%** locked. Both quotes fall outside the 4.75% ceiling band, which covers eligible deposits from one month to under six months. Describe the short-end kink and bunching from your matched quotes. A one-day cross-section does not estimate the causal impact of the cap. [VDB notice on Decision 2411/QĐ-NHNN, 12:43](https://vdb.gov.vn/tin-tuc/17062/VanBan.aspx?Type=NHPT)

[inferred] A regression could later test whether indicators predict the next monthly move in a fixed, standardized 6M household posted-rate panel. Build a point-in-time rate target from the same banks, amounts, channels and payout terms, and model monthly changes using lagged interbank and FX measures observed by the decision date. Add rate caps and deposit discontinuities as regime flags. Use a walk-forward holdout with a no-change benchmark and report absolute error, directional accuracy and interval coverage. A forecast used in the 6+6 decision would additionally need evaluation against realized six-month-ahead renewal quotes. The short matched macro sample, incomplete matched retail-rate history and lack of prospective validation support the no-forecast verdict today.

## 2. Method

### A. Exact attribution and buyer cost

[verified] One lượng (cây) is 37.5 g or 10 chỉ. One troy oz is 31.1034768 g. The conversion factor is 1.205652996 troy oz/lượng at full purity. [Hải Phòng measurement department](https://dost.haiphong.gov.vn/quan-ly-do-luong/h-o-lng-kim-hoan-1432.html) · [NIST](https://www.nist.gov/system/files/documents/2017/05/09/Precious-Metals-Conversion-Information-march2015.pdf)

[inferred] For fixed SKU, region, dealer and fineness `f`, define `G_t` in USD per fine troy oz, `E_t` in VND per USD and `W_t = G_t × E_t × (37.5 / 31.1034768) × f` in VND per gross lượng. Set `f=0.9999` for a confirmed 99.99% product. The quick calculation above uses the market convention `f=1`. The reporting convention must be visible and fixed over time. Let `A_t` be the dealer ask, `B_t` the dealer bid and `M_t` their midpoint. For any consistently defined `P_t` among those quotes, `p_t = P_t/W_t − 1` and `D_t = P_t − W_t`. The observed retail gap can be positive or negative.

```text
P_1/P_0 = (G_1/G_0) × (E_1/E_0) × ((1+p_1)/(1+p_0))
ln(P_1/P_0) = ln(G_1/G_0) + ln(E_1/E_0) + ln((1+p_1)/(1+p_0))
```

[inferred] The three terms sum in log-return units. For a VND waterfall, multiply each by `L = (P_1−P_0)/(ln P_1−ln P_0)`, with `L=P_0` when the endpoint prices are equal. The VND contributions then sum exactly to the price change. For bought-and-sold performance, specifically use `P_0=A_0` and `P_1=B_1`. The premium residual then includes the change in quote side. For a clean same-side premium series and a household waterfall, instead use mids for the three market terms and display the execution contribution separately as `ln(B_1/M_1) + ln(M_0/A_0)`. Net cash return with observable fees is `(n B_1−sell fees)/(n A_0+buy fees)−1`, for a position of `n` lượng. Do not count the spread twice.

[inferred] Instant loss per lượng is `A_t−B_t` and the share of cash spent is `1−B_t/A_t`. From today's posted bid, the buyer needs a `A_t/B_t−1` increase in the future bid to break even before fees. For the bar/ring stress test, let the chosen future ring bid be `R` and a chosen future relative bar bid gap be `d` in VND/lượng. Bar proceeds are `n(R+d)` before fees. The break-even bar bid gap is `A_bar−R` before fees. The difference in net profit versus buying the same weight of ring at its ask is `n[d−(A_bar−A_ring)]` before fee differences. Hold the ring bid fixed for one scenario and allow the buyer to change it for another. If the entry gap is zero or negative, preserve its sign. Use historically observed same-product gap quantiles only after validating both histories and count observations per regime. A full or partial narrowing is a scenario with an explicit assumption and no assigned probability.

[inferred] Join by a fixed Asia/Saigon cutoff with the last eligible quote *at or before* the cutoff and explicit quote age. A VCB historical row marked 23:00 and a US daily close can be future information relative to a same-date BTMC morning quote. If intraday matching is unavailable, use the prior completed observation as the reference, report the lag, and test the result against a consistent alternative cutoff. Keep releases separate from price timestamps. Use common trading sessions for events. Flag holidays, stale boards, stock restrictions and missing products. Exclude stale values from percentiles, avoid forward-filling policy windows and show the observation count and denominator. Pin the benchmark and data vintage. Store dealer, city, SKU, side, fineness, raw unit, normalized unit and both timestamps for each observation.

### PIT — two methods, and the ratio method is excess-only

Law 109/2025/QH15 Điều 7, as consolidated after Law 09/2026/QH16 changed khoản 1 (Văn bản hợp nhất 112/VBHN-VPQH). [verified] https://tracuuluatviet.vn/luat/luat-thue-thu-nhap-ca-nhan-109-2025-qh15/dieu-7/

- Khoản 1: at or under the Government’s revenue level, no PIT. Decree 68 Điều 4 khoản 1, after Decree 141, sets that level at 1 billion. [verified] https://vanban.org/van-ban/nghi-dinh-68-2026-nd-cp-chinh-sach-thue-quan-ly-thue-ho-kinh-doanh-ca-nhan-kinh/dieu-4
- Khoản 2, profit method: tax = (revenue − deductible costs) × rate. **The 1 billion is not deducted inside this formula.** Rates: above the threshold up to 3 billion, **15%**; above 3 billion up to 50 billion, **17%**; above 50 billion, **20%**.
- Khoản 3, ratio method, available only above the threshold and **up to 3 billion**: tax = rate × **the revenue above the khoản 1 level**. Rates: distribution of goods **0.5%**; services and construction without materials **2%** (asset lease, insurance agents, lottery agents, multi-level sales **5%**); production, transport, services attached to goods, construction with materials **1.5%**; digital entertainment content **5%**; other **1%**.
- Above 3 billion the profit method is the only method (17% or 20%).

Decree 68 Điều 4 khoản 5 repeats the choice. Điểm d: a household that elects the profit method keeps it for **two consecutive years**. A household that stays on the ratio method and then finishes a year above 3 billion must switch from the next year.

Which method binds at the cliff: the ratio method. At revenue just above 1 billion, ratio-method PIT is a few đồng. Profit-method PIT is 15% of the whole year’s profit, because khoản 2 does not carve out the first 1 billion. A household crossing for the first time can choose. It chooses the ratio method unless the year is essentially break-even. [inferred from the two formulas; no cost ratio was inserted]

Food-service PIT rate: the PIT statute does not list “ăn uống”. Thông tư 69 places ăn uống in the VAT group whose title is the same string as Điều 7 khoản 3 điểm d (1.5%). The 1.5% used below for food is that match. [inferred] Transport is named in điểm d, so 1.5% for transport is [verified]. Retail 0.5% and services 2% are the statute. [verified]

## 2. The notch, from those parameters only

Define revenue left after statutory remittances, before any cost of goods:

- If R ≤ 1,000,000,000: Y(R) = R. So Y at the line is **1,000,000,000**.
- If 1,000,000,000 < R ≤ 3,000,000,000 and the owner takes the ratio method:

Y(R) = R − v×R − p×(R − 1,000,000,000) − S

v is the VAT percentage, p the PIT ratio, S is 0 or 8,956,200.

Y(R) exceeds 1,000,000,000 only when

R > [1,000,000,000 × (1 − p) + S] / (1 − v − p)

The dominated range is every revenue from 1,000,000,001 up to the last đồng that still fails that test. As revenue approaches the line from above, PIT vanishes and the jump equals **v × 1,000,000,000 + S**.

This denominator treats every extra đồng as having no operating cost. That is the shortest range consistent with the statute. A real gross margin m replaces (1 − v − p) with (m − v − p) and makes the range longer. No official m exists, so none is plugged in.

## 4. The objections

**Sacha Dray — “it is just a calculator.”** Fatal if the screen multiplies a rate by revenue and stops. Answerable if the screen’s sentence is the dominated interval: one extra đồng assesses VAT on the whole billion (8 million for an eligible retailer today, 10 million from 01/01/2027; 40 million and 50 million for eligible services), while ratio-method PIT moves by almost nothing. A compliance economist will then ask whether owners can choose declared revenue. They can: presumptive tax has ended and the household declares. That is the mechanism. It is not evidence that they do it. Say the mechanism. Do not say the behaviour has been measured.

**Dr. Đoàn Vũ Nguyên — “no microdata”, and the public-finance point that an excess-only base is a kink.** The microdata objection is fatal to any sentence that says households bunch under 1 billion. It is answerable for a sentence that says the VAT base creates a dominated interval of a measured width and that the only public bins are 1–3 billion wide. The excess-only point is correct for PIT Điều 7 khoản 3 điểm a and wrong for VAT Điều 12 khoản 2 điểm c. A pitch that says “PIT, VAT and insurance all cliff together” loses to this judge in one question. A pitch that separates the VAT jump from the PIT kink survives it.

**Shop owner — “MISA already does this.”** Fatal if the product is a filing calculator. MISA already offers the ratio-versus-15% arithmetic (see R-DEMAND A1). Answerable if the screen shows three things MISA’s calculator is not built to show: the VAT jump next to the PIT kink, the 30-day invoice clock, and the national bins with an explicit “bunching not shown”. The social-insurance line must be worded as unresolved, or the owner is told a duty the collecting agency has not confirmed.

**“The law already uses an excess-only base.”** Partly true, and it is the most dangerous version of the pitch if ignored. PIT on the ratio method is excess-only. VAT is total-revenue once the household is inside the tax. The profit method is neither: it taxes profit on the whole revenue, and the threshold only decides whether the household is in the system. The notch survives this objection because of VAT. It dies as a story about income tax.

## 5. Verdict

**GO-WITH-CHANGES.**

Research question: for each sector, how far above 1 billion VND/year must declared revenue go before revenue left after VAT and ratio-method PIT (and, separately, after the minimum social-insurance contribution if that gate is confirmed) exceeds the 1 billion the owner keeps at the threshold?

Headline the evidence can carry: one extra đồng above 1 billion assesses VAT on the whole billion — 8 million VND for an eligible retailer and 40 million VND for an eligible service household through 31/12/2026, then 10 million and 50 million from 01/01/2027 — while ratio-method income tax does not jump. At the statutory service rate, 1.05 billion is still inside that range.

What we show if a later text made VAT excess-only too: a kink diagram (the marginal rate changes, the tax at the line is zero), the duty checklist (invoice clock, method choice, the open social-insurance gate), and the four tax-authority bins, captioned as too wide to see bunching. That product is a calculator plus a bar chart. It is the version this objection would force, and it is the weaker one. The texts read here do not force it.

Changes the build has to accept:

- Demo 1,000,000,000 against 1,000,000,001, then show the escape revenue. A jump to 1.05 billion hides the retail notch and, for services at the full rate, is still inside it.
- Two VAT columns, labelled with their end date. The cut expires 41 days after this note.
- PIT ratio as a kink. Profit method as an optional, usually worse, two-year lock. No invented margin.
- Social insurance as a conditional lump sum of 8,956,200 VND/year at the floor in force since 01/07/2026, with the 19/11/2026 “waiting for guidance” sentence on the screen.
- The Tuổi Trẻ bins as the national picture, captioned so they cannot be read as bunching. Census counts are a different population (near 5.3 million establishments versus 2.7 million tax-filers under 1 billion). Do not merge them.

# C-FTSE — FTSE inclusion, foreign trading and margin

**GO-WITH-CHANGES.** Build a replay of the FTSE inclusion windows for investors sizing exposure around future tranches. Drop the claim that retail margin funded foreign exits. Net trades identify the recorded side of a trade, while a quarter-end loan balance measures credit outstanding after flows and repayments across the quarter.

**Research question.** How did foreign net buying in the first-tranche basket differ from the rest of HOSE around the 18 September 2026 rebalance and 21 September effective session? Was the difference visible in matched trades, put-through trades, or both? Show the October 2025 announcement separately. **Supported headline [verified]** — Foreigners net bought VND 1,255bn on HOSE on rebalance Friday, net sold VND 664bn on effective Monday, and net bought VND 591bn across those two sessions (FiinQuant VNINDEX, 18 and 21 September 2026). Over 21–30 September they net sold VND 4,447bn. These are different trading windows and answer different questions.

**Sources and scope.** All linked sources were accessed **20 November 2026 (Asia/Saigon)**. [verified] denotes a directly checked source or calculation from live data. Attributed estimates preserve the publisher and scope. [inferred] marks interpretation or a proposed test. FiinQuant samples and calculations are internal.

## 3. Identification and policy

[inferred] **The proposed VN30/non-VN30 DiD does not identify the causal effect of FTSE reclassification.** Vietnam received the same country-status change. VN30 selects for liquidity and size, and its securities differ in sector exposure, foreign room and index weights. The FTSE member list also cuts across the two groups. Unexposed stocks can respond through spillovers, rotations and simultaneous benchmark changes. Announced, confirmed and implemented are different treatments, and eligibility observed in August 2026 embeds look-ahead if used to study the October 2025 announcement. The snapshot comparison above fails point-in-time assignment and provides no evidence of parallel trends. A quarterly margin stock is common to all stocks on the same date, offers no within-session identifying variation and could itself respond to the same shocks. Adding it as a control does not identify financing.

[inferred] **A narrower, repeatable test.** For each implementation tranche, freeze official GEIS additions, Frontier deletions and both benchmark weights using the last published list available before trading. Map security IDs to venue and date. Compute predicted signed net tracker demand (weight changes × pre-event tracker assets), scale it by pre-event median traded value, and compare observed matched and put-through foreign net, each scaled by pre-event median daily turnover. Compare exposed securities to same-sector, similar float, turnover, market cap and foreign-room stocks with low exposure. Publish both equal-weighted and turnover-weighted results. Chart event-time leads and pre-event group gaps over at least 60 sessions, with the August list announcement and April confirmation flagged. For October 2025, work with only contemporaneously available eligibility proxies or show market-wide description. Run sensitivity to window length (rebalance and effective days separately, then ±1 and ±5 sessions), exclusions for the largest contributors, corporate actions and other ETF schedules. Check lead estimates in levels as well as tests. A quiet pre-trend can also reflect low power.

[inferred] **Confounders and uncertainty.** The Fed raised its target **25 bp** on 16 September 2026, after HOSE’s close, so 17 September is already exposed. SBV’s seven-day FX swap/liquidity operation began 21 September according to broker coverage. Track daily USD/VND and funding conditions plus the security-specific exposure to them. Check SSC margin eligibility, which can change with warnings and trading restrictions, and use dated broker policies, such as TCBS’s large-borrower policy effective 1 October, as later context, never as explanations of earlier trades. The same rebalance Friday included other ETF/index changes. FPT went ex-rights for a stock distribution on 21 September, a reason to check corporate actions and matched-control composition. Report point estimates, coverage, a placebo-date distribution from comparable non-event weekdays, and sensitivity ranges. For any modeled interval, use date blocks to reflect market-wide shocks and serial dependence. Two macro event clusters cannot support a credible causal p-value from hundreds of stock rows. When the estimate changes sign across windows or is ordinary in the placebo distribution, label it inconclusive.
